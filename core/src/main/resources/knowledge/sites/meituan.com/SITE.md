## What it is
Meituan provides local life services such as food, food delivery, hotels, travel and movies. `www.meituan.com` currently focuses on company information and app downloads; consumer services mainly use the `i.meituan.com` mobile site and the food delivery channel.

## Pages and URLs
- Mobile site home page: `https://i.meituan.com/`, offering merchants and local life services by city.
- Another entry to the mobile site home page: `https://i.meituan.com/mttouch/page/home`, showing group buying and recommended content.
- City selection: `https://i.meituan.com/index/changecity/`, listing cities by popular cities and by the first letter of their pinyin.
- Food delivery channel: `https://waimai.meituan.com/`; the mobile food delivery domain is `i.waimai.meituan.com`.
- Food delivery help: `https://waimai.meituan.com/help/faq`.

## Features
- **Local merchants**: view information, ratings, reviews and group buying offers for restaurants and other shops.
- **Food delivery**: the food delivery channel provides delivery services for food and other goods; it is a different business from in-store group buying.
- **Hotels and travel**: provides hotel booking, travel and related ticketing entries.
- **Movies**: provides movie-related services.
- **City switching**: merchants, prices and service coverage change with the selected city or location.

## Known issues
The desktop main site and the mobile consumer site serve different purposes. Some pages only show company information or news when read, and that is no basis for concluding that the mobile site has no merchants or group buying content.

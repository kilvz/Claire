## What it is
An image and short video social platform, with personal profiles, a feed, search and explore, posts, Reels and direct messages (Messages).

## Pages and URLs
The home page is `https://www.instagram.com/`. A personal profile is `/<username>/`.

## Features
- **Profile**: username, bio, post count, follower count, following count, verification badge and private status. Display names can be duplicated; only the username is unique.
- **Posts and Reels**: author, caption, posting time, and like and comment numbers. Enter from a searched account, hashtag or post link.

## Known issues
Pages of private accounts and throttled pages all appear as empty content.

## What it is
China Railway 12306 is the official customer service website of China Railway, providing ticket search and booking, waitlists, orders, membership, station and train services, business travel services and travel information.

## Pages and URLs
- Home page: `https://www.12306.cn/`.
- Ticket search and booking: `https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/leftTicket/init`.
- Fare search: `https://kyfw.12306.cn/otn/leftTicketPrice/init`.

## Features
- **Ticket search**: the search page looks up trains by travel date, departure station and arrival station, and can filter by departure time, train type, train-set type, departure and arrival stations and seat class.
- **Search results**: the results table includes the train number, departure and arrival stations, departure and arrival times, duration, remaining tickets for each seat class and remarks; it can show discounts, points redemption and bookable trains.
- **Fare search**: the fare page lists fares for each seat class by conditions such as date and train type.
- **Personal services**: My 12306 includes train ticket orders, waitlist orders, my tickets, e-invoices, passengers, account security and other features; personal data and order features require login.
- **Waitlist**: seat classes with no remaining tickets may show a waitlist entry; waitlist orders have states such as pending payment, pending fulfillment and processed.

## Known issues
The ticket and fare pages are dynamic query pages; the initial table may be empty, and results only appear after querying. The group names for train types and seat classes may differ between the search page and the fare page. The home page mainly provides service entries; the complete train table is on the ticket search page.

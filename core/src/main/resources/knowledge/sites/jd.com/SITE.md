## What it is
The JD.com online store sells products from JD's own operation and from third-party shops; the web version provides categories, search, product details, cart, orders and a help center.

## Pages and URLs
- Home page: `https://www.jd.com/`, with product categories, search and recommended content.
- Product search: `https://search.jd.com/Search?keyword=<URL编码关键词>&enc=utf-8`.
- Product details: `https://item.jd.com/<商品SKU>.html`, where the number in the URL is the product SKU code.
- Cart: `https://cart.jd.com/`.
- Help center: `https://help.jd.com/`.

## Features
- **Search and categories**: find products by keyword or product category; the results page shows products, prices and other information.
- **Product details**: view the product information for the selected specification, and add the product to the cart.
- **Cart and checkout**: the cart supports changing quantities, selecting products and proceeding to checkout; the order confirmation page contains the recipient, payment method, invoice and delivery list.
- **Orders**: the order center under 「我的京东」 (My JD) is used to view order details.
- **Image search**: the search area on the home page has an entry for uploading or pasting an image to find the same item.

## Known issues
Pages such as search and cart may redirect to the login page; the login page offers QR code, password and SMS methods. A product SKU is not an order number.

## What it is
The QQ Music website offers a music hall, My Music, artists, new albums, rankings, category playlists, MVs, digital albums and other content.

## Pages and URLs
- Home page: `https://y.qq.com/`.
- Ranking details: `https://y.qq.com/n/ryqq/toplist/<榜单数字id>`; for example, `62` is the Soaring chart and `27` is the New Songs chart.
- Artist page: `https://y.qq.com/n/ryqq/singer/<歌手mid>`.
- Category playlists: `https://y.qq.com/n/ryqq/category`.
- MV: `https://y.qq.com/n/ryqq/mvList`; the MV chart is `https://y.qq.com/n/ryqq/mv_toplist`.

## Features
- **Rankings**: a public ranking page shows the chart name, update time, number of songs, rank, song name and artist; some charts also show rising or popularity changes.
- **Artists and playlists**: the artist page gathers the artist's songs and other information, and category playlists browse playlists by theme.
- **Playback and member content**: whether a song can be played in full depends on copyright, sign-in and membership status, and VIP and similar markers are visible on the page.

## Known issues
Much of the page content is produced by dynamic loading, so on the first read there may only be loading, sign-in or membership entries, and the chart text has not yet appeared. The numeric id in the ranking details is part of the page address, not the rank.

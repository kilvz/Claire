## What it is
A video platform.

## Pages and URLs
- Search: `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<URL-encoded query>`.
- Video: `/watch?v=<id>`; for a channel, use the channel link.

## Features
- **Videos**: search results have the title, channel, duration, view count and publish time. On the video page you can expand the description, and scrolling down loads comments. Public videos need no sign-in.
- **Channels**: name, subscriber count, About description and latest videos.
- **Subtitles**: videos with subtitles allow the subtitle text to be retrieved, and the subtitles of long videos are very long.

## Known issues
- View counts and subscriber counts are often shown with abbreviations such as 「1.2万」 (12K) and 「120K」, and exact numbers require going into the details.
- Private, members-only, age-restricted or region-restricted videos show no content, which is a different situation from no search results.
- Failure to retrieve subtitles may mean the video has no subtitles, is restricted, or there is a network problem.

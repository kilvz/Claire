## What it is
Dianping provides local merchant information for dining, leisure and entertainment, hotels and homestays, attractions, beauty, movies and shows, including reviews, recommended dishes, prices and group buys.

## Pages and URLs
- Home page: `https://www.dianping.com/`, with a city, merchant or place search box and business categories.
- Mobile site: `https://m.dianping.com/`.
- City page: `https://www.dianping.com/<city-path>`, for example `/shanghai`.
- Merchant page: `https://www.dianping.com/shop/<merchant-id>`; the mobile merchant page uses `https://m.dianping.com/shop/<merchant-id>`.

## Features
- **Search and categories**: search by merchant name or place, or enter through categories such as food, attractions, hotels and leisure entertainment.
- **Merchant information**: the merchant page can show the address, phone, business hours, per-capita spending, shop photos and user reviews.
- **Recommended dishes and menu**: dining merchant pages can show netizen-recommended dishes, the number of recommenders and the menu.
- **Group buys and discounts**: some merchants offer group-buy packages or other discounts.
- **Personal content**: near the login entry there are entries such as my reviews, favorites, group buys and table reservations.

## Known issues
The home page may only show a prompt of 「去 App 查看」 (View in app) or scanning a QR code to open the app. Merchant business status, prices and reviews change; merchants and category content differ under different city paths.

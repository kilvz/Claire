## What it is
An instant messaging service with private chats, groups and channels. The web version is at `web.telegram.org`, and the corresponding app knowledge is `telegram`. Signing in is required.

## Pages and URLs
Web version `https://web.telegram.org/k/`: search and the conversation list on the left, the chat on the right. Sign-in is by QR code, or by phone number plus verification code; with two-step verification enabled, a password is also required.

## Features
- **Reading**: the conversation list shows the last message and its time; after entering a conversation you can scroll up for earlier messages and see the sender, group members and dates. The preview in the list is not the full text.
- **Sending**: type directly in an existing conversation; for a new conversation, find the recipient from search, contacts or new messages.

## Known issues
- Groups, channels and contacts may share the same name.
- Opening a conversation may change its unread state.

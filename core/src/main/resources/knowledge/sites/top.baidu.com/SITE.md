## What it is
Baidu Hot Search is Baidu's ranking site, containing the trending list as well as category rankings for livelihood, finance, sports, entertainment, international, movies, TV series, novels and so on.

## Pages and URLs
- Rankings home page: `https://top.baidu.com/board`, aggregating trending topics, novels, movies, TV series and other content.
- Trending list: `https://top.baidu.com/board?tab=realtime`.
- Trending list rules: `https://top.baidu.com/board?page=rule&tab=realtime`.

## Features
- **Trending list**: the public page lists trending titles and hot-search indexes by rank, and some entries are marked with states such as hot or new; expanding an entry shows a summary and an entry to more content.
- **Category rankings**: different rankings show the rank, hot-search index and category information of their own entries; novel entries, for example, also have an author and a genre.

## Known issues
The official rules state that the trending list updates every 5 minutes, and that ranks and hot-search indexes change. When the website and the app are read at different times, the entry in the same position may be different.

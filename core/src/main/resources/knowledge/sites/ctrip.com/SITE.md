## What it is
Ctrip provides hotel, flight, train ticket, tour, ticket, car rental and business travel services. The home page search can find content by destination, hotel, attraction or flight number.

## Pages and URLs
- Home page: `https://www.ctrip.com/`; the mobile site is `https://m.ctrip.com/`.
- Hotels: `https://hotels.ctrip.com/`, search hotels by destination and stay information.
- Flights: `https://flights.ctrip.com/`; the domestic flight channel is `https://flights.ctrip.com/online/channel/domestic`.
- Flight schedules: `https://flights.ctrip.com/schedule/`.
- Train tickets: `https://trains.ctrip.com/`.
- Tours and vacations: `https://vacations.ctrip.com/`.

## Features
- **Hotels**: search by city, hotel or destination; results can show hotel location, price, rankings and other information.
- **Flights**: look up flights, schedules and fares; the flight channel and the hotel channel use different subdomains.
- **Train tickets**: look up trains, departure and arrival stations, times, fares and some transfer options by origin, destination and date.
- **Tours and tickets**: provide tour itineraries, independent travel, nearby trips, attractions, events and other content.
- **Community and personal content**: entries such as community, messages and mine are visible on the home page.

## Known issues
Search results and prices depend on destination, date and real-time inventory. The body of some channels is generated by dynamic loading, so a direct read may leave only the footer; this does not mean the channel has no query function.

## What it is
WhatsApp is an instant messaging app, package name `com.whatsapp`. For website knowledge see `whatsapp.com`.

## UI
There are Chats, Contacts, search and a new-chat entry. Search results are split into Chats, Contacts and Messages; contact profiles can be entered by category, or found from the Contacts list of New Chat.

## Features
- **Reading messages**: enter a conversation to read its content and times; a member you are unsure about in a group simply remains unknown.
- **Sending messages**: after choosing a contact, type the body and check the sending status in the conversation after sending.

## Known issues
- Phone-number verification and linking via browser QR-code scan are two different things.
- The interface language follows the system and is not necessarily English.

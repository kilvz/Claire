## What it is
Xianyu is a second-hand trading app, package name `com.taobao.idlefish`.

## Features
- **Products and listings**: besides second-hand goods there are personal rentals and sublets. You can see the original description, listing price, deposit and payment method, water and electricity unit prices and meter, and pet restrictions. Usually requires login. Prices and statements like 「房东本人」 (the landlord in person) are written by the poster themselves.
- **Messaging the seller**: 「聊一聊」 (Chat) on the product page enters the conversation with the seller, where you can send messages and see replies. After a message is sent, it appears in the conversation.

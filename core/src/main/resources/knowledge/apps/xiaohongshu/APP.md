## What it is
Xiaohongshu is a content and social app, package name `com.xingin.xhs`. For website knowledge see `xiaohongshu.com`.

## UI
There are usually a home feed, search, publish, messages and a personal page. Search results mix note, user, topic, location and product cards, which look similar; distinguish them by label and author.

A note's detail has an author entry, body expansion, comment section, favorite, like, share and product-association entries. The numbers on top may be engagement counts, or comment floors or product information.

## Features
- **Search**: results have a title, author, like count, cover, type and link. Usually requires login.
- **Note details**: body, author, engagement, comments. Details depend on a valid session; a detail URL you assemble yourself cannot get content.

## Known issues
The feed, popularity, prices and stock are all dynamic content.

## What it is
Zhihu is a Q&A and article community, package name `com.zhihu.android`. For website knowledge see `zhihu.com`.

## Features
- **Search**: you get the link, title and publication date of questions and articles.
- **Answers**: you can read rendered answers, the author, specific arguments and rebuttals; an answer you cannot see does not count as read. The answer author's identity, conflicts of interest and applicable model numbers should be verified separately.

## What it is
The payment and local-life services app under Alibaba, Android package name `com.eg.android.AlipayGphone`. The signed-in account holds the balance, Yu'ebao (余额宝), bank cards, bills and membership information, and it also reaches local-life services such as food delivery, transport and utility payments.

## UI
The top of the home page has shortcuts such as 「扫一扫」 (Scan), 「收付款」 (Pay/Receive), 「出行」 (Transport) and 「卡包」 (Cards); below them are the app grid and recommended content; the bottom tabs are usually Home, Wealth, Life, Messages and Me. The search box is at the top of the home page and can search by service name or merchant name.

## Features
- **Pay and receive**: entered from 「收付款」 (Pay/Receive) on the home page, which contains the payment code and the collection code; the payment code page shows a barcode, a QR code and the preferred payment method.
- **Scan**: entered from 「扫一扫」 (Scan) on the home page; it scans QR codes and can also pick a QR code image from the album.
- **Bills**: 「账单」 (Bills) under 「我的」 (Me) lists payment and receipt records by time; tapping one shows the amount, status, merchant, order number and payment method; the top can filter by month and search merchant names.
- **Balance and Yu'ebao**: the balance is visible under 「我的」 (Me); the 「余额宝」 (Yu'ebao) page shows the amount, returns and the entries for topping up and withdrawing.
- **Cards**: 「卡包」 (Cards) on the home page holds coupons, membership cards, IDs and tickets.
- **Transfers**: 「转账」 (Transfer) on the home page or under 「更多」 (More) can transfer to an Alipay account or a bank card, or find a person by phone number.

## Known issues
Entries in the home page grid and the bottom tabs sometimes share a name but lead to different channels. The payment code page refreshes its barcode and QR code after switching to the background and coming back. Bills are shown per transaction, and after a transaction is refunded there is a separate refund record.

## What it is
Kuaishou is a short video and live streaming app, package name `com.smile.gifmaker`. It also has a shop (Kuaishou stores) and direct messages.

## UI
Bottom tabs: Home, Featured, the middle 「拍摄」 (Shoot), Messages and Me.
- **Home**: the 「侧边栏」 (sidebar) button at the top left, a row of channels in the middle: Shop, Following, Discover, Local, Trending (with an edit-channels button on the right), and 「查找」 (Search) at the far right. Discover and other channels show two-column work cards.
- **Featured**: full-screen vertical videos that switch by swiping up and down. On the right are the author avatar (「关注」, Follow), like, comment, save, share and the music record, and below are the author name and the caption.
- **Messages**: a row of frequent contacts at the top, then new followers, interaction messages and the list of DM conversations, and then 「你可能感兴趣的人」 (people you may be interested in).
- **Me**: profile, followers/following/likes received; entries such as Kuaishou stores, channels you watch often, shopping group, offline mode; the tabs Works, Private, Recommended, Favorites and Likes. Search and the 「侧边栏入口」 (sidebar entry) are at the top right.

## Features
- **Search**: tap 「查找」 (Search) at the top right of the home page to enter. There are back, an input box and 「搜索」 (Search) at the top; below are search history (with delete), 「搜索发现」 (search discoveries) and 「想看的人」 (people you want to see) (which can be refreshed with 「换一换」, shuffle), and further down rankings such as hot topics, hot search people, the live ranking and the shopping ranking.
- **Home sidebar**: scan, Kuaishou stores, task center; system messages; common features (history, offline mode, settings, my wallet, drafts); tool services (official customer service, minor mode, content preferences, watch later and so on).
- **The sidebar on the 「我」 (Me) page**: my wallet, my group chats, cart, QR code, scan, share profile, offline mode, watch later, with 「管理主页」 (manage profile) and 「设置」 (settings) at the bottom.
- **Settings**: account (bind phone number, account and security, privacy settings), general (general settings, add widget, notification settings, playback settings, blocking settings, start live streaming, storage space, dark mode) and so on.

## Known issues
- After opening it may go straight to a full-screen video (Featured), and the tab read as selected at the bottom may still be 「首页」 (Home) at that point; look at the screen to confirm.
- There is often a red-envelope widget at the top left of the page (with a number and a close button) covering the content.
- The first time you enter 「消息」 (Messages) a tip introducing the group chat feature may pop up; tap 「知道了」 (Got it) to dismiss it.
- System messages are not in the 「消息」 (Messages) tab but in the home sidebar.
- The names of the tappable elements in the home sidebar are 「item_数字」 (item_number) and the text is a separate element beside them.
- The element name of the search input box is the suggested word pre-filled in the box.

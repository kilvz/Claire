## What it is
Uber Eats is a food delivery app, package name `com.ubercab.eats`.

## UI
There are Home/search, restaurant menus, Cart and Orders. Food delivery and Grocery, Convenience, Retail etc. are separate channels.

## Features
- **Restaurants**: viewed by delivery address; you can see the name, rating, reviews, estimated delivery time, delivery fee, offers, business status and link, with Delivery and Pickup modes. Restaurants that are closed have no estimated delivery time.
- **Menu**: dish names, categories, prices in the local currency, options, and whether sold out. Usually viewable without logging in.
- **Cart**: shows specs, quantity, minimum order threshold, the various fees, address and unit details and the total. Tapping Add once adds one portion.
- **Orders**: Orders has historical orders and the delivery progress of the current order.

## Known issues
- Restaurants and supermarket retail products belong to different channels.
- The interface may show login or verification.

## What it is
LinkedIn (领英) is a professional networking app, package name `com.linkedin.android`.

## Known issues
- When opened while signed out it shows the welcome page: carousel introduction cards, 「使用邮箱登录」 (sign in with email) and 「没有领英帐号?立即加入」 (no LinkedIn account? Join now).
- The welcome page may first pop up Google's 「登录LinkedIn」 (sign in to LinkedIn) bottom sheet (「继续以…的身份登录」, continue as...); this sheet belongs to Google services, cannot be read from the elements, and is closed with the 「×」 at the top right.

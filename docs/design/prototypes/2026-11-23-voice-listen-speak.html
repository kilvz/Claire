Voice + AI calls · Prototype

Like large models, it has two layers: a provider is the account you enter a key for, and a model is a specific model available under that account. One provider may offer recognition, synthesis and realtime models at the same time.

Enter each provider key once Add a provider under "Voice services" and all its recognition, synthesis and realtime models become available.
You pick models The "Listen" and "Speak" pages group models by provider: the group header is the provider, one model per row below, and the selection is a model.
Call = listen → think → speak A few seconds of silence with no new words counts as finished → the chat model produces text → it is synthesized and played in segments split at commas and periods.
Realtime voice lives under advanced Call mode defaults to "Standard"; switch to "Realtime" only when you want a realtime model that can be interrupted at any time.
